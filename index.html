03 / RESEARCH & PUBLICATION

Nanoscale semiconductor research

IEEE CONFERENCE PUBLICATION · 2022

Study of 3-nm Cylindrical GAAFETs with Variations in High-k Dielectric Gate-oxide Materials

This research examined cylindrical gate-all-around field-effect transistors at the 3-nm scale, comparing Al₂O₃, HfO₂, HfSiO₄, SiO₂, Ta₂O₅ and TiO₂ gate-oxide materials using Silvaco ATLAS. Device behaviour was evaluated using key short-channel and transistor-performance metrics.

Silvaco ATLAS Device Physics Short-Channel Effects
